# Roadmap

Where the goblin is heading. Nothing here has a date; the order is the order it’s likely to happen in.
To vote for something, or suggest something new, [open an issue](https://github.com/nickjlamb/slop-goblin/issues/new/choose)
or give an existing one a 👍.

## Now: v1.x

- **A bigger menu.** Keep adding the phrases people report. Every one comes with a test.
- **Industry slop packs.** Pharma (“patient-centric omnichannel”, “unmet need”, “paradigm-changing”), venture
  capital, HR and consulting. On by default, each with its own translations.
- **Feed him your own phrases.** A small box in the HUD for words you’re sick of, kept in your browser only.

## Next: v2

- **Browser extensions** for Chrome, Firefox and Safari. He starts by himself on LinkedIn, keeps up with the
  infinite feed, and remembers his girth between visits.
- **Slop scores.** A small sticker on each post he finishes, like a food-hygiene rating, so the worst offenders
  stand out at a glance.
- **Overeating.** Past the top girth he lies down for a nap, or bursts in a shower of the words he ate.

## Later

- **Other languages.** German, French and Spanish corporate-speak each deserve their own goblin.
- **Tuned for other sites.** He already works on any page; X, Medium and company blogs could get the same
  “… more” handling LinkedIn has.
- **Receipt themes.** Seasonal bills and a dark-mode receipt.

## Not planned

These would break the promises the goblin makes, so they won’t happen:

- **Servers, accounts, analytics or leaderboards.** Nothing leaves your browser, and that stays true.
- **Changing anything for real.** He never posts, comments, reacts, or edits what other people see.
- **Targeting people.** He eats language, not individuals. No name lists, no “roast this profile” mode.
