Take it to LinkedIn

The goblin lives in a bookmark, so it works on LinkedIn or any page you like. It only changes what you see: nothing is posted, and nothing leaves your browser. Reload to get your words back.

Bookmarks can’t run the goblin on a phone. Open this page on a laptop or desktop to install him.

Slop Goblin
  1. Show your bookmarks bar: ⌘ ⇧ B on a Mac, Ctrl ⇧ B on Windows.
  2. Drag the green button onto the bar.
  3. Open LinkedIn, click the bookmark. Esc sends the goblin home with the bill.
