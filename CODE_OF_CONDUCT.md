# Code of conduct

The Slop Goblin makes fun of a way of writing. It must never become a way of making fun of people.

## What we expect

- **Punch at the language, never at people.** Phrases, patterns and clichés are fair game. Named individuals,
  their posts, their jobs and their appearance are not, in issues, pull requests, tests, examples or screenshots.
- **Strip names before you share.** When you report slop from a real post, quote the phrase, not the person.
  Blur names and faces in any screenshot or recording.
- **Be kind to each other.** Assume good faith, disagree about the work rather than the person, and welcome
  newcomers. Nobody needs to already know regular expressions to take part.
- **No harassment of any kind.** That includes slurs, sexualised content, personal attacks, and publishing anyone’s
  private information.

## If something goes wrong

Report it privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub’s private reporting](https://github.com/nickjlamb/slop-goblin/security/advisories/new), or by opening an
issue that asks for a private contact if the report isn’t security-related. Reports are read in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and issues that break this code, and may block anyone who
keeps doing so.
